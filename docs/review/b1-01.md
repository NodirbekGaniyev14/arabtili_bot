## Grammatika
**Nuqta:** فُعِلَ / يُفْعَلُ
MAJHUL YASASH: (1) O'tgan zamon: 1-harf DAMMA + o'rtadan oldingi harf KASRA — كَتَبَ→كُتِبَ, فَتَحَ→فُتِحَ. (2) Hozirgi zamon: muzori' prefiksi DAMMA + o'rtadan oldingi harf FATHA — يَكْتُبُ→يُكْتَبُ, يَفْتَحُ→يُفْتَحُ. (3) Bajaruvchi kerak bo'lsa: مِنْ قِبَلِ + ega («tomonidan»). (4) Illatlilar: قَالَ→قِيلَ (deyildi), بَاعَ→بِيعَ (sotildi). Majhulda ASL OB'EKT ega (noibul-foil) bo'lib, raf' holatga o'tadi.
- كُتِبَ الدَّرْسُ — dars yozildi (o'tgan majhul)
- يُكْتَبُ الدَّرْسُ — dars yoziladi (hozirgi majhul)
- بُنِيَ المَسْجِدُ مِنْ قِبَلِ المُهَنْدِسِ — masjid muhandis tomonidan qurildi
- قِيلَ / بِيعَ — deyildi / sotildi (ajvaf majhuli)
