## Grammatika
**Nuqta:** س ش
س (sin) — uch tishli taroq, nuqtasiz, oddiy «s» (suv). ش (shin) — ayni taroq, TEPADA 3 nuqta, «sh» (shahar). Ikkalasi ham ULANADI va uch shaklga ega: سـ ـسـ ـس · شـ ـشـ ـش. Taroq tishlarini yozganda uchtasini ham aniq chizing.
- س — sin — uch tishli taroq, nuqtasiz — «s»
- ش — shin — taroq, TEPADA 3 nuqta — «sh»
