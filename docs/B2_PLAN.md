## 3. Dars tuzilishi (mavjud sxema, o'zgarmaydi)

`content/modules/b2/b2-NN.json` — `services/lesson_schema.py` bo'yicha:
hook → grammatika (jadval + keng tarqalgan xatolar) → o'zaklar → lug'at →
🇸🇦 hijoziy (faqat saudi-work) → 4 ko'nikma → mikro-test → SRS kartalari.

B2 nozikliklari:
- `harakat_level: "ambiguous_only"` — faqat chalkash so'zlarda harakat (real matnga o'tish);
- matnlar UZUN: `skills.reading.text_ar` 60–120 so'z (B1 da 30–50);
- `micro_test` 8 savol (B1 da 7) — K14 bank bo'laklari uchun ham zaxira;
- har darsda kamida 1 ta i'rob yoki uslub savoli.

Lug'at hisobi: nahv/sarf/balog'at 21 dars × 9 ≈ 190 · vocab-adv 10 × 14 = 140 ·
saudi-work 5 × 12 = 60 · skills 10 × 8 = 80 · takror 20 → **≈ 490 yangi so'z**.
Har so'zga edge-tts audio (~500 yangi fayl, `webapp/public/audio/b2/`).

O'zak-vazn: ~50 yangi o'zak, `content/patterns.json` ga 8 yangi vazn
(مَفْعَل مصدر ميمي، فَعْلَة اسم المرّة، فِعْلَة اسم الهيئة، فُعَيْل تصغير،
فَعالِيَّة، نسبة، أفاعل، فُعُولات).
